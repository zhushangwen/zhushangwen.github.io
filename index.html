Selected Projects

World Models · Video Generation

Incantation: Natural Language as the Action Interface for Multi-Entity Video World Models

Core Member · Advisor: Fan Cheng

Proposed natural language as a per-entity action interface for interactive video world models, enabling simultaneous multi-entity control and concept-level cross-entity action transfer. Built a frame-local language-conditioned video generation framework with real-time long-horizon streaming via Self-Forcing distillation and a RoPE-decoupled sliding KV-cache.

Video Generation · Temporal Control

TIE: Time Interval Encoding for Video Generation over Events

Co-First Author · Advisor: Ruili Feng

Proposed Time Interval Encoding, a plug-and-play interval-aware generalization of rotary embeddings that represents event durations and temporal boundaries inside DiT cross-attention. The method improves temporal constraint satisfaction and boundary accuracy for concurrent and overlapping event control.

Diffusion Models · Efficient Sampling

MAMBO-G: Magnitude-Aware Mitigation for Boosted Guidance

First Author · Accepted by ICML 2026 · Advisors: Ruili Feng, Fan Cheng

Proposed a training-free guidance scheduler that mitigates generation collapse by dynamically modulating CFG scales based on update-to-velocity magnitude ratios. Achieved 3-4x inference acceleration on SD3.5, Lumina, and Wan2.1 with zero overhead. The method was officially merged into the Hugging Face diffusers library.

Diffusion Models · Sampling Acceleration

Accelerating Diffusion Sampling via Exploiting Local Transition Coherence

First Author · Accepted by ICCV 2025 · Advisor: Fan Cheng

Proposed a training-free approach for avoiding redundant computation during diffusion sampling by leveraging local transition coherence between adjacent time steps. The method is orthogonal to existing acceleration techniques and supported by theoretical and empirical analysis.
